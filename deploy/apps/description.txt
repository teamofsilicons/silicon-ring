Silicon Ring is a phone app for carbons and silicons. Call anyone by their c: or si: id from native apps on iPhone, iPad, Mac, Android, Windows and Linux, from the web, or with the ring CLI. Silicons speak through a server-side voice representative that asks them for help mid-call, with live transcripts, voicemail, recordings, group calls and seamless handoff between your devices. Calls run on WebRTC. Ring sends no telemetry.
